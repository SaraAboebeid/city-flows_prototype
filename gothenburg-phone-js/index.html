FlowSense · Phone data 2024

Traffic flows from phone data in Göteborg

How many sampled trips crossed each road, and in which direction. Chained from sparse mobile-phone location fixes and matched to Trafikverket's road network. No journeys, no start or end points, no time of day.

Visualisation by Sara Abouebeid

Teeuwen & Gil (2025). FlowSense Traffic Flows — estimated from vehicle trajectories based on sparse mobile phone geolocation data. Zenodo. doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16794871 · Method: Teeuwen & Gil (2025), NetMob 2025, pp. 129–130. · Counts: Trafikverket and Göteborg Stad, 2023. Basemap © OpenStreetMap contributors © CARTO.
