FlowSense · Phone data 2024

Traffic flows from phone data in Göteborg

Movement estimated from sparse mobile-phone location fixes, chained into trips and matched to Trafikverket's road network. Each road counts how many sampled trips crossed it, per direction of travel.

Teeuwen & Gil (2025). FlowSense Traffic Flows — estimated from vehicle trajectories based on sparse mobile phone geolocation data. Zenodo. doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.16794871 · Method: Teeuwen & Gil (2025), NetMob 2025, pp. 129–130.
